Privacy Policy for CutMaster Pro Edge

Last Updated: May 5, 2026

1. Data Collection

CutMaster Pro Edge ("the App") does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or user information. All data is stored locally on your device.

2. Data Usage

The App operates entirely offline. All projects, measurements, and configurations are stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage APIs.

3. iCloud Backup

If you choose to enable iCloud Backup through your device settings, your local app data may be included in your personal iCloud backup. This data is encrypted and managed by Apple's iCloud services. We do not have access to this data.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No network requests are made to external servers.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or distribute any user data. The App does not contain advertising or analytics that would require data collection.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at the App Store Connect listing for CutMaster Pro Edge.
